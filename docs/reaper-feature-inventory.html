What you can do in Reaper

Everyday capabilities, in plain language — the stuff you’d demo on a sales call.

Dashboard that answers “what needs attention?”

A home base for overdue and urgent work, team load, announcements, and @mentions — so the day starts with priorities, not a scavenger hunt.

Sales angle Reduces “I didn’t see that” moments and keeps managers ahead of fire drills.

Visual schedule for the whole team

Drag-and-drop assignments across people and weeks, including recurring bookings and leave. Filter by pods (team groups). Members see their own row; managers see the full board.

Sales angle Makes staffing conversations visual and fast — perfect for weekly planning meetings.

Projects from kickoff to delivery

Organize work by client and project. Inside each project: team roster, milestones, a task board, notes, and files/links in a notebook. Star favorites so key projects stay one click away.

Sales angle Feels familiar to anyone who’s used a board tool, but tied to real schedule and budget context.

My Tasks for individual contributors

Team members get a simple view of what’s assigned to them — overdue, upcoming, and done — without wading through the full org.

Sales angle Adoption story: ICs get clarity without needing manager-level complexity.

Reports managers actually open

Utilization heatmaps across people and weeks, budget health across projects, and task rollups. Spot burnout and overruns early.

Sales angle Leadership visibility without exporting to Excel every Friday.

Clients & people directory

Keep client records and your roster in one workspace. Invite teammates, set roles, track leave, manage rates, and organize people into pods (design, eng, account, etc.).

Sales angle One system of record for “who we work with” and “who does the work.”

Reusable project templates

Save a proven delivery pattern (milestones + task structure) and spin up the next engagement without rebuilding from scratch. Export a live project into a template when you’ve nailed the process.

Sales angle Speeds onboarding of new client work and standardizes quality.

Announcements that reach the right people

Post bulletins to everyone, or target specific people and pods. Unread items show up on the dashboard — and can ping as desktop notifications.

Sales angle Internal comms without another chat tool; useful for office closures, process changes, kickoffs.

Search across the workspace

Jump to clients, projects, tasks, and comments with a quick keyboard shortcut. Searching a client also surfaces their projects. Team members only see projects they’re allowed to open.

Sales angle “Find it in seconds” — and permissions stay respected.

Who uses it — and what they see

Access is role-based so you can sell security and focus, not just features.

Member

Individual contributors

Focus on their schedule, their projects, and their tasks.

  • Dashboard, schedule (their row), and projects they belong to
  • My Tasks for personal workload
  • Search limited to projects they can open
  • Update status on work they’re part of
Manager

Delivery leads & PMs

Run the operation day to day.

  • Full schedule, reports, clients, and people
  • Project templates and org-wide search
  • “View as” a teammate to see their world
  • Public share links and holiday calendars
Admin

Workspace owners

Everything a manager can do, plus ownership controls.

  • Rename the workspace and URL
  • Change teammate roles
  • Overall workspace administration
Platform

Reaper operators

For multi-workspace / SaaS administration.

  • Create or disable workspaces
  • Control whether new signups can create workspaces
  • Enter a customer workspace when needed

Share without oversharing

Two ways to show work externally — both designed to look professional and stay safe.

Org public board

A read-only link to a curated view of the workspace (schedule, projects, reports, and more). Hide specific clients or projects you don’t want on the board. Great for internal stakeholders or trusted partners.

Client project portal

A polished, per-project page for the client: progress, team, and selected assets — without exposing internal notes, costs, or other clients’ work. Turn it on from the project when you’re ready.

Details that make demos land

Smaller features that often win the room.

Desktop app feel (PWA)

Install Reaper like an app on the desktop. Get update prompts when a new version ships, and optional desktop notifications for mentions and bulletins.

View As

Managers can temporarily see the product as a specific person — useful for coaching, support, and checking that access looks right.

Holiday calendars & leave

Build holiday calendars, apply them to people, and book leave so capacity math stays honest.

Works in the browser — demo or live

Try the product in demo mode without a backend, or connect to a live workspace with real-time updates for the team.
